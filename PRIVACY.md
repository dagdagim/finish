# Privacy Policy for FINISH - Micro-task Marketplace

**Last updated:** November 20, 2026

FINISH ("we," "our," or "us") is dedicated to protecting your privacy and personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application, FINISH, collects, uses, and safeguards information when you use our on-demand micro-task and courier platform in Addis Ababa, Ethiopia.

---

### 1. Information We Collect

When you register and use the FINISH platform, we may collect the following types of information:

* **Account & Profile Information:** 
  * Full name, phone number, email address, password (securely hashed), and optional avatar picture.
* **Task & Transaction Details:**
  * Task descriptions, task categories, pickup/delivery locations, agreed budgets (in ETB), and task completion status.
* **Location Data:**
  * With your explicit consent, we access real-time GPS location data to calculate route distances, match nearby taskers, and provide live courier tracking during ongoing tasks.
* **In-App Communications:**
  * Messages sent through our built-in real-time chat service to coordinate task details between customers and taskers.

---

### 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information solely to provide and improve the FINISH marketplace:
* Facilitate on-demand micro-task matching between customers and local taskers.
* Provide real-time delivery and errand tracking.
* Manage escrow transactions, task payouts, and balance withdrawals.
* Verify user identities to ensure platform trust and security.
* Send authentication codes (one-time passwords / OTP) to verify phone numbers or Google accounts.

---

### 3. Advertising & Third-Party Sharing

* **Ad-Free Experience:** FINISH does **not** display third-party advertisements and does **not** incorporate advertising SDKs (such as Google AdMob or Unity Ads).
* **No Sale of Data:** We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to advertisers or third-party brokers.
* **Service Providers:** We only share data with essential infrastructure providers required to operate the app (e.g., secure MongoDB cloud storage, OpenStreetMap/Google Maps for geocoding, and transactional email/SMS gateways).

---

### 4. Permissions Used by the App

* `ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION` & `ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION`: To locate pickup/delivery points and display live courier movement.
* `INTERNET` & `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`: To communicate with our secure backend API and real-time Socket.IO servers.
* `CAMERA` & `READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE`: Optional, used only if you choose to upload a profile avatar or take task completion photos.

---

### 5. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to safeguard your personal data:
* Passwords are encrypted using salted bcrypt hashing before storage.
* Network traffic is secured using HTTPS/SSL encryption.
* Session authorization uses secure JSON Web Tokens (JWT).
* Escrow funds are secured until task completion is verified.

---
